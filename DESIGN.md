# Design — Spelunky-2-hard Rogue: The Millenium Glitch

Rogue: The Millenium Glitch is a no-combat language pilgrimage as hard to beat as Spelunky 2.
The difficulty is not combat — it is permadeath, procedural chaos, and a
resource economy you cannot brute-force.

## The pillars (each maps a Spelunky-2 tension onto language)

1. **Permadeath, one run, no saves.**
   Octavia wakes in the tent under her tarp. If she breaks, that run is over
   and she starts from the tarp again. No checkpoints between NY and Nanjing.

2. **The world is never the same.**
   Every run regenerates the city. The five teachers (ro, es, zh, ru, tr) are
   placed in randomized corners. There is no memorizable route; you must
   explore, and each run demands new knowledge of the map.

3. **Three drains; any of them can end the run.**
   - **Sanity** — the dark and silence eat it. Let it reach 0 and the
     traveler breaks (run over). The only real restore is rest at a ScaiNet
     safe node.
   - **Warmth** — the cold of the street. Puddles and night drop it; trash
     and shelter raise it. At 0 you give in to the cold.
   - **Drone charge** — the little companion is Octavia's lifeline to the
     east. It drains constantly. If it hits 0, the drone is lost, and
     without it the road to Nanjing is closed (no ending).

4. **Teachers have patience; there is no retry-grind.**
   An NPC that teaches a tongue will only put up with so many wrong replies.
   Fail too many times and they walk away forever. If they leave, you must
   find another speaker — if one even exists this run. Dialogue cannot be
   brute-forced with repeated guesses.

5. **Retention decays.**
   Each tongue you learn loosens if you go too long without speaking it. If
   the thread snaps, that language is lost and must be re-earned. You must
   keep the spoken tongues alive while seeking the next.

6. **Escalating depth.**
   The five regions east of NY get harder in order: faster drains, fewer
   ScaiNet safe nodes, fewer teachers. Nanjing is the deepest, and reaching
   it means the drone must still be alive and all five tongues still in
   Octavia's mouth.

## Rules of engagement

- No combat. No health damage from enemies. The only enemy is the dark,
  the cold, silence, and the limit of Octavia's own mind.
- All difficulty flows through the resource economy + permadeath + the
  randomness of who teaches what and where.

A successful run: start at the tent → learn all 5 tongues from real speakers
→ keep them alive through decay → keep Sanity/Warmth/drone above zero →
reach a Nanjing that is only reachable if drone + 5 tongues survive.
